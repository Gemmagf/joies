# Maison Algarve

**Address.** Estrada da Ponta da Piedade, 8600 Lagos, Portugal.
**Rooms.** 148 keys: Garden Rooms (42 m²), Ocean Rooms (48 m²), Cliff Suites
(72 m²), Pool Suites (110 m² with private plunge pool), and the two-bedroom
Piedade Villa (240 m², dedicated concierge).

**Dining.**
- *Vento* — coastal Mediterranean, dinner only, open year-round.
- *Prata* — beach club and grill, lunch and dinner, April–October.
- *Terrace* — breakfast and lunch, sea-view, open all seasons.
- *Bar do Farol* — sunset cocktails, adults only after 20:00.

**Wellness.** Saltwater spa with six treatment rooms, hammam, cold plunge,
indoor and outdoor pools, and a beachfront yoga pavilion. Open 07:00–21:00.
Treatments must be booked 24 hours in advance during high season
(July–August).

**Beach.** Direct staircase access to a private cove (Praia do Farol).
Butler service on the beach 10:00–18:00 in season. Umbrellas and towels
included; paddleboards and kayaks available on request.

**Transport.** Faro Airport (FAO) is 90 minutes by chauffeur; €280 one-way
for up to three passengers. Helicopter transfers available in season on
request; €1,400 one-way.

**Character.** Coastal, family-appropriate, and slower-paced than Lisboa.
The property closes the week of 15 January for annual maintenance.
