# Airport transfers and transport

Every guest is offered a private chauffeur transfer on arrival and
departure. Prices below are per one-way trip; round-trip discounts apply.

**Maison Lisboa — Humberto Delgado Airport (LIS).**
- Distance: 8 km, ~20 minutes without traffic (30 in peak).
- Sedan (Tesla Model S, up to 3 passengers, 3 bags): €65.
- SUV (up to 5 passengers, 6 bags): €95.
- Meet-and-greet at the arrivals hall, name-card held; concierge tracks
  your flight number and adjusts for early or delayed arrivals.

**Maison Algarve — Faro Airport (FAO).**
- Distance: 90 km, ~90 minutes.
- Sedan (up to 3 passengers): €280.
- SUV (up to 5 passengers): €340.
- Helicopter transfer (in season, weather permitting): €1,400.
- One complimentary bottled water and cold towel per passenger.

**Booking.** Share your flight number at least 24 hours before arrival —
concierge@example.example — and the transfer is confirmed by return
email. Same-day requests are honoured subject to fleet availability.

**Alternatives.** Metro Baixa-Chiado is a 4-minute walk from Maison
Lisboa; the airport line runs direct from LIS in ~30 minutes for €1.85.
Faro has no direct rail link to Lagos; the taxi rank at FAO is
approximately €90–110 for the same route.

**Cancellations.** A booked transfer can be cancelled without charge up
to 4 hours before pickup. Within 4 hours, 50% of the fare is retained.
