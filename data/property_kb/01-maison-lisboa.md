# Maison Lisboa

**Address.** Rua do Alecrim 12, 1200-014 Lisboa, Portugal.
**Rooms.** 92 keys across seven categories: Classic (28 m²), Deluxe (36 m²),
Junior Suite (52 m²), Signature Suite (68 m²), Chiado Suite (85 m²),
Alecrim Suite (110 m²), and the two-bedroom Tagus Penthouse (180 m²).

**Dining.**
- *Alecrim* — Michelin-starred contemporary Portuguese, dinner only,
  Tuesday to Saturday.
- *Café Chiado* — all-day café, breakfast included for guests until 10:30.
- *Rooftop Bar* — cocktails and light plates, seasonal (April–October).

**Wellness.** A subterranean spa with two treatment rooms, a 12 m indoor
lap pool, and a hammam. Open 07:00–21:00 daily.

**Transport.** 20 minutes from Humberto Delgado (LIS) by chauffeur. Two
Tesla Model S vehicles available on request; €65 one-way city, €95 to
airport. Metro Baixa-Chiado is a 4-minute walk.

**Character.** Urban, discreet, favoured by cultural travellers and
long-stay business guests. Not designed for young children; the resort
property (Maison Algarve) is our recommendation for families.
