# Cancellation policy

Cancellation terms depend on the deposit type recorded on your booking.

**No deposit** (flexible rate).
- Free cancellation until **48 hours** before arrival.
- Within 48 hours: the first night is charged.
- No-show: the full stay is charged.

**Refundable deposit** (a deposit is held on your card).
- Free cancellation until **7 days** before arrival; the full deposit is
  refunded.
- Between 7 and 2 days before arrival: the deposit is retained; the rest
  of the stay is not charged.
- Within 48 hours: the full stay is charged.

**Non-refundable deposit** (best available rate).
- The deposit is not refundable at any time. If you cancel, the balance of
  the stay is not charged.
- Date changes are permitted once, free of charge, up to 14 days before
  arrival, subject to availability.

**Force majeure.** In the event of a documented travel restriction, a
government-declared emergency, or a family bereavement, we waive the
cancellation charge and hold the full amount as a voucher, valid for 24
months.

**How to cancel.** Reply to your booking confirmation email, use the
concierge chat, or call +351 21 000 1234. Cancellations by any channel are
timestamped and confirmed in writing within 30 minutes.
