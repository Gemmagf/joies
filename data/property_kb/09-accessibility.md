# Accessibility

We aim to make both properties usable by guests with a range of mobility,
sensory, and dietary needs. Please share requirements at booking so we can
prepare.

**Mobility.**
- Maison Lisboa: three fully accessible rooms (one Classic, two Deluxe),
  step-free entrance from Rua do Alecrim, lift to all floors. The subterranean
  spa is reached by lift; the outdoor terrace requires one step.
- Maison Algarve: nine fully accessible rooms across categories, step-free
  routes between building, restaurants, pool, and spa. The private cove is
  reached by 62 steps; a beach wheelchair is available at reception and a
  golf-cart shuttle serves the pool areas.
- Wheelchair loan is complimentary at both properties.

**Hearing and vision.**
- Portable induction loops available at reception and the two restaurants.
- Vibrating pillow alarms and doorbell flashers on request.
- Braille menus in the restaurants of Maison Lisboa; large-print menus at
  both properties.
- Guide, hearing, and assistance dogs are welcome across all indoor and
  outdoor spaces at no charge.

**Dietary.** Gluten-free, vegan, kosher (48h notice), and severe allergy
kitchens are supported in every restaurant. The pastry kitchen at Lisboa
runs a dedicated coeliac-safe workflow on Sundays.

**Contact.** Please mention specific needs when booking or write to
*acessibilidade@example.example* at least 72 hours before arrival.
