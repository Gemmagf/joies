# About the Maison

Maison is a two-property luxury hospitality brand rooted on the Atlantic coast
of Portugal. The Maison operates two distinct houses under one voice:

- **Maison Lisboa** — a 92-key urban house in the Chiado district, opened
  1971 as a residence for the founding family and converted into a boutique
  hotel in 2003. City-facing rooms overlook the Tagus estuary.
- **Maison Algarve** — a 148-key clifftop resort on the coast of Lagos,
  opened 2009. Nine pool suites, four restaurants, a saltwater spa, and
  direct access to a private cove.

Every guest is served by a dedicated *Concierge d'Honneur*, whether in
person, by phone, or through this AI concierge. Our service standard is
never to say "I don't know" without proposing who does.
