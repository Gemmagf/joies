# Check-in and check-out

**Standard check-in** is from 15:00; **standard check-out** is by 12:00.

**Early check-in** is offered without charge when the room is ready — we
prepare it as a priority when you have shared a flight number. Guaranteed
early check-in (from 11:00) is available at 50% of one night's rate.

**Late check-out** is offered by request:
- Until 14:00 — complimentary, subject to availability.
- Until 16:00 — 25% of one night's rate.
- After 16:00 — a full additional night is billed.

**Identification.** A valid passport or national identity card is required
at check-in for every adult guest. We are legally required to register
guests' identity documents with the Portuguese SEF (Serviço de Estrangeiros
e Fronteiras); the copy is destroyed after 30 days.

**Deposit at check-in.** A pre-authorisation of €200 per night is placed on
your card at check-in as an incidentals guarantee. The pre-authorisation is
released within 5–7 business days of check-out.

**Under-18 guests** must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian
across both properties. Maison Algarve accepts unaccompanied minors of 16
or older only with a signed *carta de autorização* from a parent, filed at
least 72 hours ahead of arrival.
