# Spa and wellness

Both properties operate a full spa under the *Maison Bem-Estar* programme.

**Opening hours.** 07:00–21:00 daily at both properties.
- The pool and hammam close for cleaning 14:00–15:00 on Tuesdays.
- The Algarve outdoor pool operates 07:00–20:00; heated year-round.
- The Lisboa lap pool is reserved for lengths 07:00–09:00 (adults only).

**Treatment menu highlights.**
- *Vinha Ritual* — 90 minutes, Portuguese grape-seed body treatment (€260).
- *Sal do Atlântico* — 60 minutes, salt-and-oil scrub with cliff-facing
  view (€180, Algarve only).
- *Fado Massage* — 75 minutes, deep-tissue with live-music option in the
  Lisboa Signature Suite (€290).
- *Junior Facial* — 30 minutes, age 12+, non-abrasive (€90).

**Booking.** Treatments must be booked in advance. High season (June–
September at Algarve, December at Lisboa) requires 24 hours' notice.
Weekends fill first; Sunday afternoons are typically the last-available
slots.

**What to bring.** Nothing — robes, slippers, and swimwear (unbranded) are
provided at both properties. Personal swimwear is welcome.

**Health.** Please declare pregnancy, cardiac conditions, or recent surgery
at booking; some treatments are contraindicated. Our therapists are trained
in prenatal massage from the second trimester onward.
