# Family services

**Family suitability.** Maison Algarve is our family property. Maison
Lisboa welcomes families but is a quieter, adult-leaning house — please
speak to us if you are considering a stay in Lisboa with young children.

**In-room.**
- Cribs and cots — complimentary, available in every category at both
  properties. Please request at booking or 24 hours before arrival.
- Bottle warmers, sterilisers, and baby monitors — complimentary.
- Extra bed for a child aged 2–12 — €60/night, subject to room capacity.
- Extra bed for an adult — €120/night, subject to room capacity.

**Kids club** (Maison Algarve only).
- *Ondinha* club for ages 4–11, open 09:00–18:00 daily in high season
  (June–September) and weekends year-round.
- Lunch included; supervised by two staff members with paediatric first-
  aid certification.
- €70 per child per day; guests staying 5+ nights receive one
  complimentary day.

**Dining.** All restaurants have a children's menu; the beach club at
Maison Algarve has a dedicated toddler zone. High chairs available in
every restaurant.

**Babysitting.** Available on 24 hours' notice through partner agency
CuidarBem. €35/hour, minimum three hours, cash or on-account settlement.

**Age policy.** No age restrictions across the property. The *Bar do
Farol* at Maison Algarve is adults-only after 20:00; the Lisboa Rooftop
Bar is adults-only after 19:00.
