# Pets

We welcome one dog per booking at both properties, at no additional charge.

**On booking.** Please mention your dog's name, breed, and approximate
weight at reservation. Bookings for two or more dogs are handled case by
case; ask before booking.

**In the room.** A Maison pet bed, water and food bowls, and a welcome
biscuit are placed in the room ahead of arrival. Dogs must not be left
alone in the room; we can arrange a walker on 4 hours' notice (€25/hour).

**Where dogs are welcome.**
- All guest rooms and suites.
- The lobby, reception, and gardens at both properties.
- The Rooftop Bar at Lisboa (until 19:00) and *Terrace* at Algarve
  (breakfast and lunch), on the outdoor seating only.
- The Algarve beach cove (leashed).

**Where dogs are not permitted.**
- The spa, pool decks, and treatment rooms.
- The interiors of *Alecrim*, *Vento*, and *Prata*.
- The Ondinha kids' club and its terrace.

**Assistance dogs.** Guide, hearing, and other assistance dogs are welcome
across all spaces (see [Accessibility](kb-accessibility)) at no charge.
