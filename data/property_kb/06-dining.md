# Dining

**Reservations.** Restaurants at both properties fill quickly; we recommend
reserving 48 hours ahead. In-house guests are prioritised. Concierge can
also secure tables at partner restaurants in Lisboa (Belcanto, 100 Maneiras)
and Lagos (A Barrigada, Bahia Beach) — usually within the same day for
lunch, longer lead time for dinner.

**Dress code.**
- *Alecrim* (Lisboa dinner): jacket for gentlemen; no denim, no shorts.
- *Vento* (Algarve dinner): smart casual; sandals and beachwear not
  permitted after 19:00.
- *Prata* (Algarve beach club): swimwear with cover-up at lunch; smart
  casual at dinner.
- *Café Chiado*, *Terrace*, and *Rooftop Bar*: no dress code.

**Breakfast.** Complimentary for guests on a bed-and-breakfast rate
(check your booking confirmation), served 07:00–10:30 at both properties.
À la carte and traditional Portuguese buffet.

**Dietary requirements.** All restaurants can accommodate gluten-free,
vegan, kosher (with 48h notice), and nut allergies. Please flag any
allergy at reservation and again with the maître d'hôtel on arrival at the
table. Kitchens are not certified allergen-free environments.

**In-room dining.** 24 hours at both properties. The full restaurant menu
is available 12:00–22:00; a curated late-night menu after that.
