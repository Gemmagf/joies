# Curated experiences

The concierge offers a small, curated set of experiences at each property.
Every experience is bookable at least 24 hours ahead; prices are per
person unless indicated.

**Maison Lisboa.**
- *Fado & Supper in Alfama* — a private table at a family-run *casa de
  fado*, chauffeured from and to the hotel. €220. Runs Wednesday to
  Saturday.
- *Chiado at Dawn* — an hour walking the empty streets with a Lisboa-born
  urbanist, breakfast at Café A Brasileira on return. €140. Daily.
- *Ceramics Atelier* — a half-day at a working *azulejo* studio in
  Marvila, take home a hand-painted tile. €280. Tuesday to Friday.

**Maison Algarve.**
- *Sunset Sail* — private catamaran along the Ponta da Piedade cliffs,
  with a chilled bottle of Alentejo white. €480 per couple. Runs
  April–October, sea state permitting.
- *Grotto Kayak at Dawn* — guided kayak into the caves at first light,
  before the day-boats arrive. €190. Wednesday, Friday, Sunday.
- *Chef's Table on the Cliff* — a private six-course tasting menu from
  chef Vento, served at a single table on the cliff terrace. €340. Two
  seatings per week, subject to weather.

**Both properties.**
- *Douro Vineyard Day* (Lisboa) or *Alentejo Vineyard Day* (Algarve) —
  chauffeured private tour with a Master of Wine, three-winery visit,
  lunch included. €650. Tuesday and Thursday.
- *Wine Cellar Tasting* — 90 minutes in the hotel cellar with the
  sommelier, four glasses. €120. Daily, must be booked before 15:00.

Experiences suggested by the concierge are ranked by predicted affinity,
not by margin. If a proposal doesn't fit, say so — the concierge will
requery.
