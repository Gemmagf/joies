# Sustainability

The Maison operates under the *Maison Verde* environmental programme.
Highlights below; the full annual report is published each March.

**Certifications.**
- Green Key: renewed annually at both properties since 2018.
- Portugal Sustainable Tourism: level "Excellence" (Algarve, 2023;
  Lisboa, 2024).

**Energy.**
- 100% renewable electricity (Portuguese grid, EDP guarantees of origin).
- Rooftop solar at Maison Algarve covers ~40% of daytime pool heating.
- Room thermostats default to 22 °C; deviations are respected but the
  concierge will follow up after 3 nights if the room is left at extremes.

**Water.**
- Greywater from spa pools is filtered and used for garden irrigation at
  Algarve. Lisboa is on municipal water only.
- Bathroom amenities are in refillable ceramic dispensers, not sachets.

**Sourcing.**
- All fish served at Algarve is line-caught within 40 km of Lagos; the
  restaurant closes the octopus dish on the two months of the annual
  seasonal ban.
- Wine list emphasises Portuguese producers; ~85% of bottles at both
  properties are Portuguese.

**Community.**
- We employ 218 people; 94% live within 30 km of the property they work in.
- Annual partnership with the Instituto de Turismo de Portugal to host
  eight hospitality apprentices across the two properties.
